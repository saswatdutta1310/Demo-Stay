# Step-by-Step Demo Presentation Script

**Goal**: Show hotel owners how much 15-20% OTA commission (MakeMyTrip, Booking.com) they save by giving guests an effortless direct-booking experience.

---

### Step 1: The Problem Hook (1 minute)
> *"Namaste [Owner Name]. Today, when guests find you on Google or WhatsApp, they often still book via OTAs because they want instant booking confirmation. That costs you 18-20% commission on every room night."*

### Step 2: Show Their Personalized Brand (2 minutes)
- Open `https://[client-slug].demo-stay.com`.
- Point out their exact logo, colors, nearby landmarks, and real room rates.
- Highlight the **WhatsApp Direct Chat button** and **Instant Confirmation Promise**.

### Step 3: Run a Live Booking Checkout (3 minutes)
1. Select Check-in and Check-out dates.
2. Select the Deluxe Room.
3. Fill guest details.
4. Click **Proceed to Pay** -> Opens Razorpay test checkout.
5. Enter test card details (`4111...`) or test UPI.
6. Boom! Instant Confirmation voucher page appears with unique Booking Code.

### Step 4: Show the Owner Portal (3 minutes)
- Navigate to `/owner` and enter PIN `1234`.
- Point out the real-time notification: *"Look, the booking we just made appeared instantly on your calendar and your payments feed without any middleman cut."*

### Step 5: Close with Pricing & 48h Turnaround
> *"We can connect your real bank account and launch this live on your custom domain in 48 hours."*
